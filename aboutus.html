Oleksiy Kulinich

In 2002 graduated from Kirovograd State Technical University in agronomy.

In 2007 defended his thesis on "Research and creation of source material for breeding lentils, adapted to the northern steppes of Ukraine."

Since 2007 is a senior researcher in the laboratory breeding of cereals and legumes in the Institute of Agriculture steppe zone NAAN of Ukraine.

Research interests:
the study of species diversity of  lentils,
development of new varieties with higher yield, disease-resistant, suitable for mechanical harvesting.
