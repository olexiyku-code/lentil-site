Lentil seeds


Short description
The vegetation period is of 65-75 days. The plants are bushy, compact, erect, 54-55 cm tall. The seeds are flat, light-green. The weight of 1000 seeds 58-70 g. The pods are flat, rhombic, 17-18 mm long and a width of 9-10 mm, with one or two seeds in a pod. The yield is 2160 kg/ha The food quality is excellent. The protein content in the seeds is of 27%.
Variety Linza is recommended for cultivation in the steppe and forest steppe zones of Ukraine.
