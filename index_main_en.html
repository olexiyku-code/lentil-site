    The basic growing season of most crops coincides with the period with an air temperature above 10 ° C. In the northern part of the Steppe of Ukraine, 20 years ago, the average long-term average daily temperatures became over 10 degrees Celsius observed after April 22 in the spring and until October 7 in the autumn. The duration of the period with temperatures above 10 ° C was 170 days. The average sum of active temperatures higher than 10 ° C during this period was 1200-1500 ° C. In recent years, with an average annual temperature increase of 1.5 ° C, from 7.6 ° C to 9.1 ° C, this period has increased substantially. The average daily temperatures now became over 10 degrees Celsius after April 14 in the spring and until October 17 in the fall. The duration of the period with temperatures above 10 ° C is now 188 days. The average sum of active temperatures higher than 10 ° C during this period increased to 1500-1800 ° C.
Along with the increase in extreme weather events (such as drought, heat, tornadoes, etc.), one of the consequences of global warming is the expansion of the range of crops that can now be grown in Ukraine. Nowadays, as previous studies show, crops such as mung bean, vigna mungo, adzuki bean, pigeon pea (kayanus), which were previously grown mainly in subtropical latitudes, can now be grown almost all over Ukraine.
